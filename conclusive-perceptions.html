    Zone A

    External Sources Survey

    We treat a conclusive perception as a durable convergence: a statement that survives scrutiny across fields. Each carries a confidence tier: Strong (replicated, widely accepted), Moderate (supported but contested), or Interpretive (dependent on worldview), with its support and, where one has been identified, its strongest counterpoint.

    1. First-person experience is not captured by third-person description alone.

      ◇ Philosophy Moderate

      Support: Chalmers 1995; SEP: Consciousness; Nature, 2023

      Strongest counterpoint: Illusionism and other physicalist accounts treat the intuition of a gap as the thing to be explained (SEP: Consciousness).

    2. Strict, simple materialism faces serious unresolved problems, and there is no consensus theory of consciousness.

      ◇ Philosophy ◆ Science Strong that no consensus exists; Moderate on the limits of materialism

      Support: SEP: Physicalism; Nature, 2025

      Strongest counterpoint: Promissory physicalism: the explanation will come with more science.

    3. Mind and brain are tightly coupled.

      ◆ Science Strong

      Support: Koch et al. 2016; workspace and integrated-information studies (Chapter 04)

      Strongest counterpoint: Coupling is not identity: dualists accept the coupling and read it differently (SEP: Dualism). This perception holds under every metaphysics.

    4. Awareness can occur without behavioural responsiveness.

      ◆ Science Strong for covert awareness in patients; Moderate for consciousness during cardiac arrest

      Support: Monti et al. 2010; AWARE II, 2023

    5. Transformative, meaningful experiences are common across traditions and are reported reproducibly, in mystical states and near-death experiences.

      ◆ Science ✦ Faith Moderate

      Support: James, Varieties; Griffiths et al. 2006; van Lommel et al. 2001; AWARE II

      Strongest counterpoint: Science documents the reports and their themes of peace, meaning and connectedness; their interpretation is a matter of faith.

    6. Transcendence-seeking and moral formation, especially compassion, are central in every major contemplative tradition.

      ✦ Faith Interpretive

      Support: SEP: Mysticism; SEP: Buddha; SEP: Daoism; Catechism

    7. Neither science nor philosophy currently decides whether we survive death.

      ◆ Science ◇ Philosophy Strong, as a statement of what is known

      Support: SEP: Afterlife; SEP: Death

    8. Epistemic humility: say what kind of claim is being made.

      ◇ Philosophy Strong

      Support: SEP: Religion and Science; adversarial collaboration as method

    9. Practices change people, with measurable physiological and psychological effects, without those effects deciding the metaphysics.

      ◆ Science Moderate

      Support: Davidson et al. 2003; Wager et al. 2004; Kaptchuk et al. 2010; STEP, 2006

    10. AI systems can discuss the soul fluently, but their statements are not independent spiritual evidence; whether any machine has experience is unresolved.

      ◇ Philosophy ◆ Science Strong, as a statement of what is known

      Support: Chapter 08, sections B and D

    11. Moral agency under extremity can be studied empirically, but the worth it defends is a matter of philosophy and faith.

      ◆ Science ◇ Philosophy ✦ Faith Interpretive

      Support: Oliner rescuer research (MIDUS summary); SEP: Conscience; Yad Vashem

      Strongest counterpoint: Empirical “extensivity” among rescuers is a Science finding; claims about the non-interchangeable worth of each human being are Philosophy and Faith.

    Zone B

    Independent Research Conclusions

    Written under an instruction to follow the evidence, not to please anyone. Bias is possible in researchers and in AI; see Chapter 08.

    Three lenses, one person

    Science tells us how consciousness is coupled to the brain; philosophy tells us what that coupling can and cannot mean; faith, including observation and experience, tells us what a life of meaning looks like. No lens may overrule another outside its own kind of claim.

    1. ◆ Science ◇ Philosophy Consciousness is real, first-person, and not yet explained by any single scientific theory.
    2. ◆ Science The link between mind and brain is strong. ◇ Philosophy ✦ Faith What it implies about a soul remains a matter of argument and of faith.
    3. ✦ Faith Convergent human testimony across mystical, near-death and contemplative traditions points toward meaning, connection and transcendence. We treat it as significant observation and experience, not as proof.
    4. ◇ Philosophy Immortality is the central open question.
    5. ◇ Philosophy Humility and compassion are the practical conclusion, whatever one’s metaphysics.

    Moral agency at the edge

    ◆ Science Rescuer research distinguishes an empirical trait, “extensivity,” an expanded sense of responsibility toward strangers (MIDUS summary of the Oliner study).

    ◇ Philosophy Whether each threatened human being has non-interchangeable worth is not settled by that finding; it is a philosophical claim.

    ✦ Faith The conviction that saves a life when silence would be safer is faith made visible: a culture of life (hanscalmeyer.com; Yad Vashem).
