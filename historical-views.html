    Two tracks through history

    That each position below was held is a matter of historical record History. What each position claims about the soul is labelled as philosophy or faith.

    Classical and Christian

    1. Plato ◇ Philosophy
      The soul is immortal and a prisoner of the body (Phaedo; SEP: Plato).
    2. Aristotle ◇ Philosophy
      The soul is the form of the body (De Anima; SEP: Aristotle's Psychology).
    3. Plotinus and Neoplatonism ◇ Philosophy
      All things emanate from the One, and the soul returns to it (SEP: Plotinus; Neoplatonism).
    4. Augustine ✦ Faith
      An immaterial soul, an inner self, memory and will (SEP: Augustine; Confessions; City of God).
    5. Thomas Aquinas ✦ Faith
      The soul is the substantial form of the body; it subsists, survives death, and is reunited with the body at the resurrection (SEP: Aquinas; Summa Theologiae I q.75; q.76).
    6. René Descartes ◇ Philosophy
      Substance dualism: mind and body are two distinct substances (SEP: Dualism).
    7. Modern Christian thinkers ◇ Philosophy
      Nancey Murphy defends non-reductive physicalism (Bodies and Souls, or Spirited Bodies?); Richard Swinburne (The Evolution of the Soul) and J. P. Moreland (The Soul) defend substance dualism, discussed in SEP: Afterlife. Overview: Christian materialism.

    World traditions

    1. Vedanta ◇ Philosophy
      Śaṅkara's Advaita: the Self is Brahman (SEP: Śaṅkara).
    2. Buddhist Abhidharma ◇ Philosophy
      The person analysed into momentary elements without a permanent self (SEP: Abhidharma).
    3. Yogācāra ◇ Philosophy
      The “consciousness-only” school (SEP: Yogācāra).
    4. Dharmakīrti ◇ Philosophy
      Buddhist epistemology of mind and perception (SEP: Dharmakīrti).
    5. Ibn Sīnā (Avicenna) ◇ Philosophy
      The “Flying Man” thought experiment on self-awareness (SEP: Ibn Sīnā; IEP: Ibn Sīnā).
    6. al-Ghazālī ✦ Faith
      SEP: al-Ghazālī.
    7. Suhrawardī ◇ Philosophy
      The philosophy of illumination (SEP: Suhrawardī).
    8. Ibn ʿArabī ✦ Faith
      Sufi metaphysics (SEP: Ibn ʿArabī; Sufism).
    9. Mullā Ṣadrā ◇ Philosophy
      SEP: Mullā Ṣadrā.

    Core concept · home chapter

    Faith Manifestation

    Definition. How faith becomes visible: in action and works, transformed character, practices such as prayer, meditation and worship, and claimed providential or healing outcomes. One rule holds throughout: measured effects of practices ◆ Science are not evidence for or against a divine cause ✦ Faith.

    ✦ Faith

    Faith without works is dead (James 2:14–26); “work out your own salvation” while God works in you to will and to act (Phil 2:12–13). Augustine's writing on grace and will is the classic church-history anchor (see the Confessions and City of God above). On claimed providential outcomes: SEP: Miracles.

    ◇ Philosophy

    What faith is (belief, trust, or commitment): SEP: Faith; Pascal's Wager; Hope; Religion and Science.

    ◆ Science

    What practice measurably does, from a large randomized trial of intercessory prayer to meditation, placebo and immune studies, is set out on Chapter 04.

    Life Saver · a culture of life

    Faith made visible in costly protection

    History Hans Georg Calmeyer acted in secrecy, with no reward or public recognition during his lifetime. He died in 1972 and was recognized posthumously by Yad Vashem as Righteous Among the Nations in 1992 (Yad Vashem; hanscalmeyer.com).

    ✦ Faith Faith manifestation at its sharpest edge is conviction embodied in costly protection of another human being when silence would be safer: a culture of life set against a totalitarian culture of death.

    His work as a lawyer inside the occupation is told on Chapter 03.
