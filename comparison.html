    Core concept · Immortality, continued

    Arguing about survival

    ◇ Philosophy Philosophers have long debated whether death is the end, from Epicurus’s claim that death is nothing to us to the view that death harms us by depriving us of goods (SEP: Afterlife; SEP: Death; SEP: Personal Identity).

    ◇ Philosophy Plato’s Phaedo gives the classic arguments for the soul’s immortality (Phaedo); dualism keeps survival conceivable (SEP: Dualism).

    ◇ Philosophy Transhumanists propose technological continuity and mind uploading (Transhumanism; Mind uploading). Questions of identity and personhood for artificial systems are discussed in SEP: Ethics of Artificial Intelligence and SEP: Artificial Intelligence. Cryonics and uploading remain speculative engineering proposals, not demonstrated.

    The worth of a soul

    ◇ Philosophy Treating a single threatened life as “a universe” asserts that human worth is not interchangeable. See the two-legged foundation and hanscalmeyer.com.
